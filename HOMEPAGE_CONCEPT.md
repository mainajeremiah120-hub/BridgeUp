# BridgeUp Homepage Concept

## Goal

The homepage should make BridgeUp feel like a live global social platform.

Users should immediately see:

- What interests are trending
- Which rooms are live now
- What people are discussing
- Which communities are active
- Who they may want to meet
- Which mentors are online
- Which events are happening now

The homepage should not present BridgeUp as a country, city, university, or
department directory.

## First Impression

The first screen should communicate:

```text
BridgeUp is where I find people around the world who care about the same things
I care about.
```

The page should feel active, populated, and international.

## Hero

### Headline

```text
Find your people by interest, skill, and ambition.
```

### Supporting Text

```text
Join global communities, live rooms, discussions, mentors, events, and
collaborators around what you care about.
```

### Primary Action

```text
Explore Interests
```

### Secondary Action

```text
Join Live Rooms
```

### Hero Background Feel

The hero should include visible signs of activity:

- Floating interest tags
- Live room previews
- Member avatars
- Online indicators
- Discussion snippets
- Global city/country hints as secondary metadata

Avoid using a static institution hierarchy as the main visual.

## Homepage Layout

Recommended order:

1. Hero with live activity preview
2. Trending Interests
3. Live Now
4. Popular Discussions
5. Top Communities
6. People You May Like
7. Mentors Online
8. Events Happening Now
9. Top Contributors

## Logged-Out Homepage

Logged-out users should see enough real activity to understand the product.

Allowed previews:

- Trending interests
- Public communities
- Public discussions
- Live room titles
- Mentor previews
- Event previews

Require signup for:

- Joining rooms
- Posting replies
- Messaging people
- Following users
- Joining private communities
- Requesting mentorship

## Logged-In Homepage

Logged-in users should see a personalized dashboard.

Personalized modules:

- Continue where you left off
- Live rooms in your interests
- New discussions in your communities
- People matching your goals
- Mentors for skills you want to learn
- Events in your interests
- Skill exchange matches
- Nearby activity, optional

Example:

```text
Today on BridgeUp
3 rooms are live in AI
12 new posts in Programming
4 people want to learn React from someone like you
2 mentors are online for Cybersecurity
```

## Discovery Filters

Filters should be available but not dominant.

Primary filters:

- Interest
- Skill
- Goal
- Language
- Profession
- Online now

Secondary filters:

- Country
- City
- University
- Organization

This makes location useful without making it the core product.

## What To Remove From The Homepage

Remove homepage focus on:

- Country -> City -> University -> Department hierarchy
- Example university chain
- Directory-first language
- University-first mentorship language
- Institution-based identity as the main entry point

## What To Add To The Homepage

Add homepage focus on:

- Global interest graph
- Live rooms
- Trending discussions
- Top communities
- Recommended people
- Mentors online
- Events happening now
- Top contributors
- Optional location filters

## Design Feel

The homepage should feel like a modern social product:

- Dynamic activity modules
- Online indicators
- Avatars
- Interest tags
- Live counters
- Clear join actions
- Search-first discovery
- Dense but readable content

It should not feel like a marketing-only landing page. The product experience
should be visible immediately.
